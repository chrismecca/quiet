#+TITLE: Wash derivation evidence
#+SUBTITLE: Why Wash displaces its ground in OKLab
#+SETUPFILE: setup.org

* Purpose

quiet-core defines Wash as a displacement of the local ground in OKLab,
toward the hue of a chromatic family, by a fixed distance.  The line tier
preserves the ground's lightness.

This report records the evidence for that definition: the alternatives that
were rejected, the gamut limit on the line distance, and the reason Field is
the canonical ground.

* Requirements

A wash marks a region, such as a diff line or a diagnostic line, with the
meaning of one chromatic family.  The line tier must:

1. carry the family's hue visibly;
2. keep every foreground that is readable on Field readable on the wash;
3. give all four families the same perceptual strength;
4. remain inside sRGB without clipping.

In the tables below, /lowest/ is the lowest contrast among the foregrounds
that must reach 4.5:1: Muted and the four chromatic anchors.  Ink, at 7:1,
is never the binding constraint here.

* Rejected: interpolation toward the anchor

Selection is derived by interpolating from Structure toward Mineral in
OKLab.  The same operation from Field toward each family anchor was
evaluated first.

#+CAPTION: Lowest 4.5-class contrast on Field interpolated toward each family.
#+ATTR_LATEX: :font \small
#+INCLUDE: "../data/wash-interpolation.org"

The chromatic anchors are readable foregrounds, so they are far from Field
in lightness.  Interpolating toward them mostly darkens quiet-day rather
than tinting it.  quiet-day falls below 4.5:1 at every amount evaluated.

quiet-night tolerates small amounts, but one definition must serve both
adaptations.  Interpolation fails requirement 2.

* Rejected: fixed absolute chroma

The second alternative kept Field's lightness and set a fixed absolute
OKLCH chroma at each family's hue.  The table shows the resulting distance
from Field.

#+CAPTION: Distance from Field at a fixed chroma of 0.025.
#+ATTR_LATEX: :font \small
#+INCLUDE: "../data/wash-fixed-chroma.org"

quiet-day Field is warm: its own hue lies near Ochre.  At a fixed chroma,
Ochre barely moves away from Field while Mineral moves several times as far.
Fixed chroma fails requirement 3.

* Chosen: displacement by a fixed distance

The chosen rule adds a fixed-length vector in OKLab's chromatic plane,
pointing along the family's hue, to the ground.  Lightness is unchanged in
the line tier, so distance from Field is identical for every family by
construction.

#+CAPTION: Canonical line washes over Field.
#+ATTR_LATEX: :font \small
#+INCLUDE: "../data/wash-displacement.org"

Every family moves the same distance, and the lowest 4.5-class contrast
stays above the floor in both adaptations.  Requirements 1 to 3 hold.

* Gamut limit

A displacement can leave sRGB.  quiet-core treats that as a derivation
failure rather than a color to clip.  The table gives, for each family, the
largest line distance over Field that remains in gamut.

#+CAPTION: Largest in-gamut line distance over Field.
#+ATTR_LATEX: :font \small
#+INCLUDE: "../data/wash-gamut.org"

One line distance is shared by all families, so it is bounded by the
smallest ceiling in either adaptation.  quiet-day Rust sets that bound.
The chosen distance of 0.020 leaves headroom below it.  The test suite fails
if any ceiling falls to or below the line distance.

* Canonical ground

A wash depends on its ground.  The table evaluates every family's line wash
over each architectural background.

#+CAPTION: Lowest 4.5-class contrast over line washes on each ground.
#+ATTR_LATEX: :font \small
#+INCLUDE: "../data/wash-grounds.org"

Over Field, both adaptations pass.  Over quiet-day Surface and quiet-night
Structure, the lowest foreground falls just below 4.5:1.

Field is therefore the canonical ground, and the contract evaluates washes
over Field.  A port that places a wash on another ground must derive it
over that ground and verify the foregrounds it actually draws there.

* Emphasis tier

The emphasis tier marks a span inside a line wash and is intended for Ink.
It moves lightness away from the ground so that it can carry more chroma
within gamut.

quiet-core requires only Ink at 7:1 on each emphasis wash.  Those results
appear in the conformance report.

* Independence

The canonical wash values asserted by the =internal/core= tests were
computed by an independent implementation of the specified rule, not by the
reference implementation.  The tables here document the reference
implementation; the fixtures check it.
